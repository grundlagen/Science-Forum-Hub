# Prospectus logic

The objective is not to maximize anomaly count. It is to find leads with a plausible path from public signal to independently corroborated, legally material evidence.

A useful ranking framework is:

`rule fit × evidence strength × cross-tender persistence × material-addition potential × unresolvedness × timeliness ÷ obviousness-to-other-miners`

For U.S. opportunities add explicit gates for federal payment/claim, certification or representation, scienter, materiality, public-disclosure/original-source risk and first-to-file collision.

Current calibration lead: `leads/pstcl_ganpati_shivdevi_2026.md`. It is valuable because a procurement authority reportedly detected a shared submission IP in one tender and the same pair appear L1/L2 in an adjacent tender from the same division. It is not currently a strong monetary prospect for the user; its purpose is to calibrate the detector before applying the same structure to U.S. federal-aid procurement data.

The Oregon DOT screen is the first U.S. corpus pass. State DOT bid tabulations are especially useful because federal-aid rules require State DOTs to prepare detailed bid tabulations for FHWA, giving a relatively rich losing-bid dataset rather than awards alone.

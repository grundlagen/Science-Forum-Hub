# Falsification checklist

Before promoting any procurement anomaly to a serious lead, attempt to disprove it.

- Confirm bidder identity with stable identifiers; reject name-only joins.
- Check declared affiliates, JVs, consortia and common ownership.
- Check whether the authority supplied a common template or re-rendered submissions.
- Check whether a common consultant, accountant, bond agent, subcontractor or authorized tender agent explains shared metadata/IP.
- Compare the candidate pair against the full market baseline: bid-gap distribution, submission-time distribution and item-price correlations.
- Check geography and market concentration: repeated co-bidding may be inevitable in a thin local market.
- Verify every anomaly against the original procurement portal or stage document rather than an aggregator when possible.
- Search protests, debriefs, audits, enforcement actions and bidder explanations for benign explanations.
- Record contradictory evidence in the lead packet.
- For U.S. relator prospects, separately establish the representation/certification, federal money or claim, materiality, scienter evidence, public-disclosure/original-source risk, first-to-file collision risk, and limitations.

A high anomaly score alone is never evidence of fraud.

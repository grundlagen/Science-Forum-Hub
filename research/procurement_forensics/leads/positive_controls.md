## Shared-consultant / disclosed-collaboration control
The Santa Paula Union Oil Building RFP proposal set is useful as a false-positive control: public materials show that at least some separately submitted proposals were openly coordinated/complimentary rather than independent competitors. A document-provenance detector should not promote such a cluster without first resolving disclosed partnerships.

Control principle: metadata or linguistic commonality becomes more meaningful only after excluding agency templates, common authorized agents, disclosed teaming and portal-generated transformations.

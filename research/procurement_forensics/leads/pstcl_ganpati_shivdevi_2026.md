## Important falsification / data-quality points

1. **Same IP is not itself proof of collusion.** Possible benign explanations include a common tender consultant, shared office/network, authorized submission agent, cybercafe, ISP NAT, or affiliation/JV not yet resolved.
2. `GANPATI CONSTRUCTION` is a generic Indian contractor name. Entity resolution is incomplete. Do not join this bidder to similarly named firms in Madhya Pradesh, Assam, Rajasthan, etc. without GST/PAN/address or portal bidder ID.
3. TenderKart's TE 6 rendered line-item amounts do not reconcile perfectly with its displayed total bid for SHIVDEVI (the visible item extensions sum to a different figure). Treat ₹748,100 / ₹749,100 as portal-mirror reported totals pending the official BOQ comparison file.
4. We have **not** established that TE 6 submissions came from the same IP. The same-IP finding is confined to TE 5 unless portal records show otherwise.
5. SHIVDEVI has broader PSTCL bidding history, including a 2025 technical rejection on an unrelated tender and a 2026 AC-installation award at Nakodar. That makes it less plausible that it exists only as a disposable cover bidder, but does not resolve the relationship with GANPATI.

## Prospectus

**Investigative value:** medium-high as a positive-control / cross-tender linkage lead.

**Current evidentiary value:** medium. The procurement authority itself reportedly identified a same-IP relationship in one tender, while a closely adjacent tender from the same division had the pair ranked L1/L2 only 0.134% apart.

**Direct monetary / relator route:** presently weak. This is an Indian public-procurement lead, not a U.S. FCA lead. Its principal value to this project is as a real-world calibration case for detecting the same structure in U.S. federally funded procurement, where false independent-bid certifications and federal payment could have a more relevant relator pathway.

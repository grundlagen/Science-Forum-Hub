# Scoring notes

The pair score is deliberately heuristic and used only for triage. It increases with repeated co-bidding, sub-1% and sub-0.5% total bid gaps, recurring L1/L2 adjacency, both members winning across their shared tender history, and near-perfect line-item price correlation across enough items.

Future versions should replace fixed weights with empirical null distributions stratified by buyer, work type, geography, contract size and bidder count. A feature's value should depend on rarity in its procurement ecosystem, paralleling the rarity weighting used by the document workshop fingerprinter.

# U.S. target corpora

Priority is on procurement systems that preserve losing bids and can implicate federal funds.

1. Oregon DOT bid item history (2024-2026) — automated screen in this branch.
2. MnDOT awarded-contract abstracts — public CSV/PDF contains all pay items, contractor bids and engineer estimate.
3. TxDOT bid tabulations — all submitted project bids plus engineer estimate; federal project identifiers can be joined when available.
4. Caltrans Contract Cost Data — millions of historical bid-item observations and bidder filters.
5. WSDOT bid tabulations — contract-level bid item/contractor histories.

Promotion criteria for a U.S. prospect: repeated non-random pair pattern + exact legal entity resolution + federal-aid nexus + relevant independent-bid/collusion certification + evidence materially beyond a statistical screen.

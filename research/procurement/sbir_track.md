# Parallel prospectus track: SBIR/STTR overlap

Reason for inclusion: cross-program contradictions may create a stronger federal False Claims Act prospectus than obvious public procurement similarity. Recent DOJ/SBIR enforcement has included allegations of duplicate/overlapping work and payroll charged across federal programs.

Proposed detector:

1. Pull SBIR/STTR award title, abstract, firm/UEI, PI, agency, award dates and performance period.
2. Pair awards to the same firm/PI across different agencies and overlapping periods.
3. Rank semantic overlap in title/abstract/research keywords and deliverables.
4. Link publications, patents, public repositories and technical reports to detect reused outputs.
5. Where pandemic-period awards overlap, compare PPP timing only as a lead; do not infer duplicate payroll without payroll/application evidence.
6. Falsify disclosed related work, sequential Phase I/II continuation, explicitly non-overlapping work packages, common platform technology and properly allocated personnel.

Promotion requires a concrete pair of awards whose representations/deliverables materially conflict after these benign explanations are tested.

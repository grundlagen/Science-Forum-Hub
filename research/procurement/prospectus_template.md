# Prospectus template

## Identified target transaction
Agency; solicitation; award; bidder(s); date; amount.

## Primary evidence
Original bid/application/claim files and exact provenance.

## Anomaly
Specific contradiction or improbable common-origin pattern.

## Independent corroboration
Corporate graph, pricing, permits, awards, technical output, archive, or other independent stream.

## Falsification performed
Common templates; portal rewrite; consultant; joint venture; channel pricing; rebates; legitimate subcontracting; disclosed related work; other benign explanation.

## Claim/payment nexus
What federal or other legally relevant claim/payment was actually made; do not infer from anomaly alone.

## Knowledge/materiality
What is known, what is inferred, and what evidence is still missing.

## Novelty/legal obstacles
Public disclosure; original source; first-to-file; limitations; already-open investigation.

## Missing evidence / next decisive test
State the cheapest test that could kill or substantially strengthen the lead.

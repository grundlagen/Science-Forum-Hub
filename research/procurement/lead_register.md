# Procurement forensics lead register

Status date: 2026-09-08

This register records **screening leads and controls, not allegations of wrongdoing**. Promotion requires primary-document corroboration and explicit innocent-explanation testing.

## CONTROL — Town of Davie, Florida, ITQ JA-20-93

Publicly exposed competing responses: TransValue, Brink's, MidFlorida; bid tabulation and award materials are also public.

Initial price check: line totals reduce coherently to the stated pickup/unit rates. Nothing in the public tabulation presently supports collusion or false bidding. Keep as a useful negative/control corpus if original binaries can be retained intact.

Why useful: three nominally independent bidder documents from one solicitation, plus tabulation/award outcome.

Missing: locally preserved original binaries for provenance extraction.

## CONTROL — Santa Paula Union Oil Building RFP

Five proposals are publicly listed. At least one proposer openly discloses collaboration with other proposal participants, including a separately submitted proposal. Any document/text similarity must therefore be downweighted or explained before common-origin inference.

Why useful: strong falsification example for the 'shared workshop' detector.

## CONTROL — Tonopah High School Sports Complex public GitHub archive

Rich collection of subcontractor proposal text and project records. Many forms were printed through the same project-management environment at the same displayed time. Shared structure/time is therefore a workflow artefact, not evidence of bidder coordination. Do not use as a positive case without independent signals outside the common system.

## LEAD — unidentified 2026 Cisco government procurement

A public contractor discussion reports an exact-brand/model Cisco/ACI government solicitation in which the poster bid about $502k and says the award was about $348k, below their claimed ~$450k wholesale hardware cost. Poster says the winner's bid was obtained by FOIA.

Current status: **unidentified and unverified**. Exact-dollar and Cisco/ACI searches have not yet resolved the procurement. Do not attribute to any bidder or agency.

Innocent explanations already identified in the discussion: Cisco deal registration / special bid pricing, surplus or new-old-stock equipment, back-end rebates, state/enterprise discounts, strategic loss-leading, differing channel costs. The lead should be discarded unless the actual solicitation, winning bid, BOM and authorized-channel pricing can be reconciled and still leave a material contradiction.

## Promotion checklist

A candidate becomes a prospectus only if it has most of:

1. Identified solicitation, agency, bidders and award.
2. Original bidder files or authenticated copies.
3. Multiple rare provenance similarities, not ubiquitous software/template features.
4. Persistence across separate tenders, or a second independent evidentiary stream.
5. Corporate/ownership/address/employee/subcontractor graph checked.
6. Pricing pattern checked against legitimate volume discounts, deal registration and common suppliers.
7. Solicitation template / portal / agency-side scanning/re-rendering falsified.
8. Public complaints or chatter converted into testable assertions rather than treated as evidence.
9. Award/payment/claims trail established if an FCA theory is contemplated.
10. Public-disclosure, first-to-file, limitations and scienter gaps recorded explicitly.
